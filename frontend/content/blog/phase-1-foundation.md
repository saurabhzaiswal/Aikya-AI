# Building Aikya AI from a focused foundation

Aikya AI starts with a simple promise: help people understand important content across languages without hiding the product's boundaries.

## What Phase 1 includes

The first working release focuses on two core workflows that already create immediate value: text translation and readable translations of supported digital PDF documents. It also includes account access, private workspace boundaries, and a small workspace activity view so the experience stays explainable and manageable.

## What waits

OCR for scanned pages, layout-preserving document rendering, billing, voice, mobile, desktop, browser extensions, and optional AI assistance remain outside this phase. They will only be considered after the core workflow is useful, dependable, and clearly validated by real users.

## Why the boundary matters

For a solo-founded product, maintainability is part of user experience. A smaller, explicit system is easier to secure, explain, operate, and improve. That is why the first launch is intentionally tight in scope: it demonstrates the translation workflow without overpromising the rest of the future product stack.

## The current product posture

Phase 1 is best understood as a trustworthy MVP, not a full platform promise. It tells the user exactly what is available today, which documents are supported, and what privacy and operational assumptions are in effect. That clarity is part of the product itself.

## Why the public story matters

A calm product story is easier to trust than a long list of future claims. If the foundation is honest, scoped, and privacy-aware, it becomes much easier for the product to grow in a way that users can understand and review.
