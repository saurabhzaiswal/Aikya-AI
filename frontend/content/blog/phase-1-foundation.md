# Building Aikya AI from a focused foundation

Aikya AI starts with a simple promise: help people understand important content across languages without hiding the product's boundaries.

## What Phase 1 includes

The first working release focuses on text translation and readable translations of supported digital PDF documents. It also includes account access and a small workspace activity view.

## What waits

OCR for scanned pages, layout-preserving document rendering, billing, voice, mobile, desktop, browser extensions, and optional AI assistance remain outside this phase. They will only be considered after the core workflow is useful and dependable.

## Why the boundary matters

For a solo-founded product, maintainability is part of user experience. A smaller, explicit system is easier to secure, explain, operate, and improve.
