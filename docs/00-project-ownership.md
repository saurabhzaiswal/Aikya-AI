# Project Ownership

Product: Aikya AI  
Founder: Saurabh Choudhary  
Ownership model: Independent SaaS product

Author, maintainer, and license owner: Saurabh Choudhary. Official public developer identity: [saurabhzaiswal on GitHub](https://github.com/saurabhzaiswal).

The founder owns product decisions, technical direction, branding, business strategy, privacy policy, and release approval.

AI agents act as technical, documentation, implementation, and architecture-review assistants. They must follow repository instructions, expose trade-offs, preserve decision history, and request approval for material changes. They do not own intellectual property or decision authority.

Architecture, technology, data/privacy, provider, business, and brand changes require founder approval. Local reversible implementation choices may proceed when they conform to approved requirements and ADRs.

The project goal is to build Aikya AI into a sustainable global language-intelligence SaaS that remains understandable and operable by its solo founder.
