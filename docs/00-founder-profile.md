## Vision

Saurabh is building Aikya AI as an independent global language-intelligence platform intended to remove language barriers and make information understandable without sacrificing document structure, privacy, or user control.

## Development model

- Solo-founder product and engineering leadership.
- AI-assisted architecture, documentation, implementation, and review.
- Open-source technology and cloud infrastructure.
- Evidence-led, incremental SaaS delivery.

## Responsibilities

The founder retains authority for product vision, architecture, engineering direction, business model, brand, user experience, privacy policy, and long-term strategy. AI agents provide analysis and implementation assistance; they do not own decisions.

## Engineering philosophy

Build deliberately and correctly. Prefer clean architecture, documentation, security, maintainability, accessibility, and solo-founder operability. Do not build a temporary demo; build a product capable of becoming a durable company.
